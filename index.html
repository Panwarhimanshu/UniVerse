FAQ

Questions consultants ask first

What exactly is UniVerse?＋
UniVerse is a B2B value added services marketplace for immigration consultants and education agents. It consolidates nine student services — education loans, Canada GIC, German blocked accounts, forex remittance, forex cards, insurance, OSHC, accommodation and international SIM cards — into a single platform, so you deal with one partner instead of nine vendors.
Will my student data be shared with competing consultants?＋
No — this is the core design principle of the platform. Student records submitted by you are ring-fenced to your account and are never visible to, or shared with, another consultant on the platform. UniVerse is a service provider, not a recruitment competitor.
Which countries does UniVerse support?＋
Fifteen study destinations: Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, New Zealand, Ireland, the UAE, Singapore, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Sweden, Japan and Spain. Individual services vary by destination — GIC is Canada-only, OSHC is Australia-only — while loans, remittance and insurance span all fifteen.
How do I earn from UniVerse?＋
You earn a commission on every completed service. Payouts trigger on defined events — a funded account, a disbursed loan, an activated SIM, a paid premium — and are credited to your in-platform wallet, withdrawable to your bank account at any time.
How do I become a partner?＋
Register through the partner portal with your agency name, contact details, zone and city. Once verified, you get dashboard access to submit student service requests, track applications live and view your commission wallet.
How long do services take to process?＋
It varies by service: same day for insurance and OSHC certificates, 2–3 working days for fee remittance, 3–5 days for blocked accounts and SIM delivery, 5–7 days for Canada GIC, and 7–14 days for education loan sanction.
